## English (en-US) — varsayılan

**App name (≤30):** Life Path: Life Simulator

**Short description (≤80):**
Live a whole life from birth to old age — through 100+ mini-games.

**Full description:**
One life, a thousand choices.

Life Path is a life simulator where you don't just tap buttons — you actually play your life. Study for exams by solving real questions, score a header in the big match, diagnose a patient, haggle at the market, fix a pipe or trade on the stock market. Every path has its own mini-games.

🌱 FROM CRADLE TO OLD AGE
Grow up in a poor, middle-class or wealthy family, in a village or a big city. Your talents, temperament and family shape your start, but your effort decides where you end up.

🎮 118 MINI-GAMES
Football, basketball, table tennis, parallel parking, surgery, triage, coding, circuits, farming, cooking, trading and many more. Your character's skill makes them easier, but your own skill matters too.

📚 REAL EXAMS
Report cards every year, high school and university entrance exams, driving test, civil service exam, medical specialty exam — thousands of generated questions on maths, science, history, geography, literature and languages.

💼 26 CAREERS, ONE LADDER AT A TIME
Doctor, engineer, software developer, footballer, teacher, police officer, lawyer, banker, portfolio manager, farmer, craftsman… Doors open one by one: interviews ask questions about your field, and promotions come with performance.

📈 MONEY THAT FEELS REAL
Salaries, income tax, rent and living costs, inflation, a stock market with 22 stocks and funds, savings accounts, loans and mortgages, houses and rental flats. Start a tiny business at school and grow it into an exporter — or go bankrupt and climb again.

🃏 LIFE HAPPENS
216 event cards — common, rare, epic and LEGENDARY. Family ups and downs, friendships, love and marriage, children, luck and misfortune. Legendary chances only appear in the areas you've worked for.

👪 GENERATIONS
When your life ends, it becomes an album. Continue as one of your children with the family name, reputation and business you built.

No gambling, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win. Effort, wisdom and honesty pay off. Optional rewarded ads only.
